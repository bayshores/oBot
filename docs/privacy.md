# openbot privacy policy

*Last updated: November 6, 2026*

openbot is an app you run on your own computer. It lets AI assistants ("Bots") that run on that
computer help you with tasks, including your email and calendar if you connect them.

## What openbot can access

When you connect a Google account (Gmail and Google Calendar) or a Microsoft account (Outlook mail
and calendar) in **Settings › Connections**, openbot can read, search, draft, send and organize
your email, and see and change your calendar events. It only does this when one of your Bots needs
to for something you asked, and only for the Bots you allow in Settings › Connections.

Sending email, inviting people to events and deleting always ask you first.

## Where your data goes

- **Your computer.** openbot runs on a computer you control. The sign-in it keeps for your account
  (a refresh token) is stored there, encrypted. Chats are stored there too.
- **Anthropic.** Bots are powered by Claude through your own Claude account. When a Bot reads an
  email or a calendar event to help you, that content is sent to Anthropic's Claude service as part
  of the conversation, under your Claude account and Anthropic's terms.
- **Nobody else.** The makers of openbot don't run a server that receives your data. openbot doesn't
  sell, share or use your data for advertising, and doesn't use it to train AI models.

## Google user data

openbot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is only used to provide the features you use
in openbot, and people never read it unless you ask openbot to show it to them.

## Removing access

- In openbot: **Settings › Connections › Remove** deletes the saved sign-in (for Google, openbot also
  tells Google to revoke its access).
- Any time from your account: [Google third-party access](https://myaccount.google.com/connections)
  or [Microsoft app permissions](https://account.live.com/consent/Manage).
- Deleting openbot's data folder on your computer removes everything it stored.
